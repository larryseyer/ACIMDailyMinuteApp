## Title

Help with ACIM Daily Minute

## Intro

ACIM Daily Minute is a free daily reader for *A Course in Miracles*. There are no ads, no accounts, and no tracking.

If something in the app is broken, or you cannot find a reading, this page is how to get help.

## How to reach us

Email: **use the contact address published on [acimdailyminute.org](https://www.acimdailyminute.org/about.html)** (put the live address here when you publish the page).

Please include:

- which device you are using (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, or Apple Watch)
- what you tapped
- what you expected to see
- what you saw instead

## Common questions

### The app is free. Is there a catch?

No. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. The Course text in this app is the Sparkly Edition, which is public domain in the United States.

### Do you collect my data?

No. The app does not collect analytics, crash reports, or advertising data. There is no account with us.

Your highlights, notes, and saved readings stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud in Settings. If you turn that on, those marks live in **your** iCloud, which we cannot read.

Full policy: [Privacy](https://www.acimdailyminute.org/privacy.html)

### A reminder did not fire.

Reminders are set on your device, not on a server.

1. Open the app → Settings.
2. Confirm the Daily Minute reminder and the Daily Lesson reminder are on, each at the time you want.
3. On iPhone: Settings → Focus. Reminders do not arrive during Focus unless you allow this app.
4. On iPhone: Settings → Notifications → ACIM Daily Minute. Allow notifications.

### Something I saved on my iPhone is not on my Mac.

iCloud sync is off until you turn it on.

1. On each device, open Settings in the app.
2. Turn on iCloud under Your Work.
3. Use the same Apple ID on both devices.
4. Wait a minute, then open the Saved tab again.

If you never turned iCloud on, a backup file from Settings → Backup & Restore will still carry your marks to the other device.

### A day on the Minute shelf is empty.

On Video’s Minute shelf, some days were missed when the daily passage was first published. Missed days are filled in one per night. The empty day tells you when that one is due. You can also tap **Let it fall open** for another published minute.

### A Video row has no YouTube recording.

The row still plays. The app shows the passage on the screen — the art and the words, with narration when there is audio. Apple TV always does that; it has no YouTube player.

### Video on the Listen tab will not play.

The list of audio episodes still works. If you are offline, or YouTube is down, the app says the video is unavailable and leaves the audio list in place.

Apple TV has no YouTube player. On the television, use Listen for audio.

### I deleted the app and my highlights are gone.

Highlights, notes, and saved readings live on the device (and in your iCloud only if you turned that on). Deleting the app removes the on-device copy.

If you made a backup file first (Settings → Backup & Restore), import that file after you reinstall.

## What this app is not

This app does not represent the Foundation for Inner Peace. It does not use FIP’s later copyrighted editions. It is an independent companion to the Sparkly Edition of the Course.
