## Description

ACIM Daily Minute delivers a daily passage from A Course in Miracles paired with that day's Workbook Lesson, on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple TV. Each minute is drawn from the Sparkly Edition — also called the Sparkle, Thetford, or Hugh Lynn Cayce (HLC) edition — and presented exactly as written, without commentary or interpretation.

Open the app and today's minute is waiting. A short passage to sit with, reflect on, and carry into your day. That's it — one minute, one lesson, every day.

FEATURES

- Today: today's daily minute and Workbook Lesson, refreshed automatically
- Read: the Workbook spine and the Text; search by number or keyword; jump to any lesson
- Listen: podcast feeds for daily minutes and lessons, plus embedded YouTube audio
- Archive: calendar of every past daily minute, searchable by date or text
- Saved: bookmark any passage; swipe to remove
- Reminders: a Daily Minute reminder and a Daily Lesson reminder, each at your own time, and practice reminders that follow the Workbook lesson you are on, inside your own waking hours — with a custom chime
- Widgets: small, medium, and large Home Screen and Mac widgets showing today's minute
- Live Activity: Dynamic Island and Lock Screen presence while today's minute is active
- Apple Watch: today's minute on the wrist, with three complication styles and phone-to-watch sync
- Apple TV: today's reading as a player on the television
- Siri Shortcut: "What's today's reading?"
- Deep links: today's minute, any lesson, any archive date, or Saved
- Optional iCloud: highlights, notes and bookmarks can stay in your own Apple account — off unless you turn it on

LINEAGE

The text is the Sparkly Edition of A Course in Miracles, associated with Teddy Poppe and the Course in Miracles Society (CIMS). It is based on the 1972 manuscript edited by William Thetford with Helen Schucman. That edition is treated as public domain: a U.S. federal court (S.D.N.Y., 2003–2004) invalidated copyright on the 1975 edition because copies had been distributed without a copyright notice, and publishers of the Sparkly Edition have distributed it as copyright-free. This app does not use the Foundation for Inner Peace's later copyrighted English editions, Clarification of Terms, supplements, or translations. It is an independent companion and does not represent or claim affiliation with any organization.

PRIVACY

ACIM Daily Minute collects no data whatsoever. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, no ad networks, no user accounts, and no tracking of any kind. Network requests go only to acimdailyminute.org to fetch daily content. Highlights, notes and bookmarks stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync in Settings; then they travel between your Apple devices through your own iCloud private database, which we cannot read. Cached passages never leave the device. Zero third-party SDKs — only Apple's built-in frameworks.

This app is offered freely, in the spirit of the Course itself.

**Declaration: Data Not Collected.**

ACIM Daily Minute does not collect any data from users. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no ad networks, no user accounts, no device fingerprinting. Optional iCloud sync stores highlights, notes and bookmarks in the reader's own iCloud private database; the developer cannot read it, so it is not data collected by the app. Verified against Info.plist (no NSUserTrackingUsageDescription, no ATTrackingManager, no AdServices framework) and source code (zero third-party SDK imports).
